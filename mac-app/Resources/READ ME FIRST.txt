soulget for Mac
===============

Type an artist, a record label or an album. soulget reads the full track list
from Discogs, removes duplicates, and downloads one copy of each track from
Soulseek. Nothing to set up: soulget makes its own Soulseek login.

Install
  1. Drag "soulget" into your Applications folder.
  2. Open it. The first time, macOS may say it can't check the app for
     malicious software (soulget isn't sold through Apple). Then:
       - macOS 13 or 14: right-click soulget in Applications, choose Open,
         then Open again.
       - macOS 15 or later: try to open it once, then go to
         System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down and click
         "Open Anyway".
     You only do this once.

Use
  - Type a name in the box at the top and press Return.
  - Click Get next to the right artist, label or album. The arrow next to
    Get has more choices (originals only, no compilations, or "later").
  - Music goes to the Music folder, in "soulget" (change it in Settings).
  - Missing tracks: soulget can look again every 7 hours by itself
    (Settings > General). Who has which file depends on who is online.
  - Closing the window keeps soulget running in the menu bar (the record
    icon at the top of the screen), so downloads carry on.

Settings (soulget menu > Settings)
  - General: music folder, quality (lossless first is the default), the
    7-hour check.
  - Soulseek: the automatic login, or your own Soulseek name.
  - Discogs: an optional free key, for pictures and faster reading.

Needs macOS 13 (Ventura) or later, on a Mac with an Apple chip or Intel.
